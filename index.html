Power rank by week

Power score (out of 100) is 50% all-play win %, 30% average score over the last 3 weeks, and 20% official win % (bonus wins included). Each component is scaled from the league's lowest to highest before weighting. Change is spots gained (+) or lost (−) since the previous week. Tap a line or team to highlight it.

Chance of finishing in each place

Odds of making the playoffs, week by week

Odds come from 10,000 simulated seasons run after each week. Each simulation plays out the rest of the real schedule using every team's scoring average and spread (blended with the league's early in the season) and awards bonus wins to each week's top 5 scorers. Places are in the official standings, with points for as the tiebreaker.
